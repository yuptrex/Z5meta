About

Hi, I'm Zem.

I move between two layers most people keep separate: the firmware that talks directly to hardware, and the software people actually touch. I ship both, solo.

How I work

On the bench, that means ESP32 and Arduino builds — reading sensors, driving actuators, debugging with a multimeter when the logic analyzer isn't enough. In production, it means shipping web apps and Android apps end to end: architecture, backend, UI, deployment, and the infra that keeps it all running.

I run everything solo — from Firebase security rules to Render deploy configs to key rotation. Most projects are single-developer builds from first commit to shipped product, which shapes how I scope and structure things: dependency-light, pragmatic, and built to actually ship rather than sit half-finished.
